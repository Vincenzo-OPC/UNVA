# Community Code of Conduct

UNVA welcomes contributors who want to improve AI fluency and make routine work more efficient.

Be respectful to other participants, including virtual assistants, business owners and people whose work is changing. Critique workflows and business models without harassment, threats, doxxing, or demeaning individuals. Share original or permissioned material, protect privacy, and disclose conflicts of interest.

Maintainers may moderate or remove abusive contributions and block participants who repeatedly violate these rules. Report concerns through the repository's private contact routes when possible.
